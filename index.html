(04) — About Hewa Holding

From a humble trading outfit to a formidable regional player.

HEWA Holding of Companies was formed to consolidate the businesses operated by the group under Chairman Mr. Ahmad Kadir Ismail Al Majidi. Today it is a transnational conglomerate spanning construction, contracting, commodity trading, retail, residential cities, entertainment parks, hospitals, petroleum and petrochemical trading, a plastic factory and real estate.

With high-profile partnerships with multinationals and an extensive network across business communities, financial institutions and government, Hewa Holding offers the accessibility of a local firm with the strength, stability and resources of a regional corporation.

History

A story written in Erbil.

Over the years the group diversified and grew — from commodity trading into investment projects, real estate development, construction and retail, within Erbil and abroad.

  2. 1950

    Born in Erbil

    Founder Mr. Ahmed Qadir Al Majidi is born in Erbil.

  3. Roots

    A trading house

    Mr. Al Majidi starts his career in the commodity trading business, while also handling various other interests of the family.

  4. Growth

    Diversification

    The group expands into investment projects, real estate development, construction and retailing — within Erbil and abroad.

  5. 2009

    Majidi Mall & Majidi Park

    Majidi Mall opens its doors on 23 November 2009, and Majidi Park is founded as a leisure park for the citizens of the Kurdistan Region.

  6. 2014

    Majidiland

    Majidi Park becomes Majidiland — credited as the first amusement park in the country for its dimensions and magnitude.

  7. 2015

    Wholly owned

    Majidiland becomes wholly and privately owned by Hewa Holding.

  8. Today

    A regional player

    1,000+ staff, operations and investments across Kurdistan, the GCC countries and Turkey — with Grand Majidi Mall on the way.

✦ New project

Erbil · Shaqlawa Road

Grand Majidi Mall

A comprehensive cultural and commercial experience that bridges the old and new, the oriental and western, the modern and conventional.

The mall is located on the north-eastern skirts of Erbil on Shaqlawa Road, in a city that grows radially from its centre to the outer rings. Erbil is a retail-centric city: commerce plays a significant role, with malls, shops, restaurants and historical bazaars that also support the city's tourism.

The project sits in the physical development direction of the city — growing fast and urbanising — and will become a new urban destination not only for Erbil's citizens but also for local tourists: an urban marketplace full of experiences that attracts visitors of all ages, interests and backgrounds.

Shoppers are lured outside of the interior mall into the daylight and a new sequence of spaces, forming a new public heart for Erbil City.
