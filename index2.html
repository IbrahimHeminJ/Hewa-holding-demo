01 — About Hewa Holding

From a humble trading outfit in Erbil, Hewa has grown into a formidable regional player — with operations and investments across Kurdistan, the GCC and Turkey.

HEWA Holding of Companies was formed to consolidate the many businesses operated by the group under its Chairman, Mr. Ahmad Kadir Ismail Al Majidi.

Born in Erbil in 1950, our founder started his career in the commodity trading business while handling the family's other interests. Over the years the group diversified into investment projects, real estate development, construction and retail — within Erbil and abroad.

Today the Group spans Construction, Contracting, Commodity Trading, Retail, Residential Cities, Entertainment Parks, Hospitals, Petroleum & Petrochemical Trading and Manufacturing — backed by high-profile partnerships with multinationals and an extensive network across business communities, financial institutions and government.

By bringing multi-disciplinary teams together, HEWA Holding offers partners and customers a single point of responsibility, accountability, coordination and communication — the accessibility of a local firm with the strength, stability and resources of a regional corporation.

    04 — New projects

    Grand Majidi Mall

    Coming to Erbil

    A new public heart
    for Erbil City.

    Located on the north-eastern skirts of Erbil along the Shaqlawa road — in the direction the city is growing — Grand Majidi Mall is a comprehensive cultural and commercial experience that bridges the old and new, the oriental and western, the modern and conventional.

    Erbil is a retail-centric city of malls, shops, restaurants and historical bazaars. This urban marketplace, full of experiences for visitors of all ages, interests and backgrounds, will be a destination for Erbil's citizens and the region's tourists alike — luring shoppers out of the interior mall into the daylight and a new sequence of open spaces.
